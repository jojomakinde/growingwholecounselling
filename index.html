Ways we can work together

Counselling looks different depending on who you are, what’s happening, and what you need. Here are some of the areas we can work on together.

Adults

Anxiety · Stress · Trauma · Grief · Self-esteem · Relationships · Life changes

Maybe you're overthinking everything, carrying something from the past, struggling with a relationship, or just tired of feeling the way you've been feeling. We’ll get clear on what’s going on, understand what’s feeding it, and work on practical ways to manage it differently.

Explore your fit

Couples

Communication · Conflict · Trust · Boundaries · Disconnection · Relationship changes

Maybe every conversation turns into an argument, one of you shuts down while the other pushes to talk, or you love each other but haven't felt like a team lately. We’ll look at the pattern you’re getting caught in and work on what you can both do differently.

Explore your fit

Children Ages 5–13

Anxiety · Big emotions · Anger · Behaviour · Confidence · Friendships · School

Kids don't always sit down and tell us exactly what's bothering them. Sometimes it comes out in their behaviour, worries, meltdowns, anger or withdrawal. We use play-based approaches, creative activities and practical skill-building to help children express what’s going on and learn ways to handle big feelings.

Explore your fit

Teens & Youth Ages 14–17

Anxiety · Self-esteem · Emotions · School stress · Friendships · Family · Relationships

Being a teenager comes with a lot of opinions about what you should be doing and who you should be. Counselling doesn't need to feel like one more adult giving you advice. We can talk about what’s actually going on, figure out what matters to you, and work on tools that are useful in your actual life.

Explore your fit

Parents & Caregivers

Emotional regulation · Behaviour · Anxiety · Parenting stress · Boundaries · Communication

Sometimes you know your child is struggling, but you genuinely don't know what to do when the meltdown, anger, anxiety or shutdown happens. We help parents understand what may be driving the behaviour and, importantly, what to actually do in those moments.

Our counselling approach

01

We understand the why

We want to understand what’s underneath what you’re feeling, thinking or doing, not just focus on the surface.

02

We keep it practical

Understanding is important, but so is knowing what to do next. We’ll work on tools and strategies you can actually use in real life.

03

We work together

You know yourself and your life better than anyone. We’ll bring our clinical experience, ask questions, offer a different perspective, and we’ll figure things out together.

04

We’ll be honest with you

We’ll support you, but we’ll also be honest when we notice a pattern, something isn’t working, or there may be another way to look at it. Always with care, never judgment.

05

Counselling doesn’t have to feel so serious

Some sessions are heavy. Some have plenty of laughter. Usually, there’s room for both. You can expect warmth, humour and a little personality in the room too.

06

Your circle of care matters

When helpful and with the appropriate consent, we can collaborate with others involved in your care, such as schools, physicians and other professionals, so the support doesn’t have to stop at the counselling room.

Approaches we draw from

Depending on what you need, our work may include Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Solution-Focused Therapy, play-based approaches, attachment-based approaches, trauma-informed care, emotion regulation work, and psychoeducation.

Faith can also be incorporated into counselling when it’s important to you. It’s always your choice.

Insurance

Using insurance?

Many extended health benefit plans include coverage for counselling provided by a Registered Social Worker (MSW, RSW). We’ll provide you with a receipt that you can submit to your insurance provider.

Because every plan is different, please check with your insurance provider to confirm that Registered Social Worker services are covered under your plan. Growing Whole Counselling does not currently offer direct billing.

Need immediate support?

Growing Whole Counselling is not a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent help, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department. In Canada, you can also call or text 988 for suicide crisis support.
