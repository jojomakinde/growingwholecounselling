Support that feels human

There are no “bad” people—only stories, needs, and patterns worth understanding.

The goal is not to reduce you or your child to a label. It is to understand the whole person and build from the strengths already there.

My counselling approach

Insight that meets real life.

My approach is collaborative, practical, and tailored to you. I draw from CBT, ACT, DBT, Solution-Focused Therapy, and trauma-informed care to help us understand your experience and identify tools that feel realistic for your life.

Meet your counsellor

Hi, I’m Josephine.

Josephine Adeosun, MSW, RSW · Social Worker & Counsellor

I work with adults, youth, children, couples, and caregivers navigating all kinds of seasons in life. I believe there is always more to a person than what they are struggling with, and counselling should be a place where you can be honest without feeling judged, rushed, or reduced to a diagnosis.

Together, we’ll make sense of your experiences, build on your strengths, and find practical ways to move forward. You do not have to know exactly what you need before we begin—we can figure that out together.

About insurance

Itemized receipts can be provided for counselling services from a Registered Social Worker (MSW, RSW). Coverage varies by plan and is not guaranteed, so please confirm eligibility directly with your insurer before booking.

Need immediate support?

Growing Whole Counselling is not a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department. In Canada, call or text 988 for suicide crisis support.
